About BRD.

Rio de Janeiro · Niterói · Campinas · California

We are a computational design studio working between design and computation.

We work alongside architecture practices, engineering firms and builders to solve what conventional drawing can't reach: forms that are hard to build, decisions that need data, and processes that repeat on every project.

Our work always starts from the model. The same parametric base is used to analyse, optimize, document and fabricate, which cuts rework and keeps the design consistent from study to site.
