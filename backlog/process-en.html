How we work

From model to decision, in five steps.

The same process applies to every service. We work on the files your team already uses (DWG, RVT, IFC or 3DM) and deliver back in the same format.

  1. Step 1

    Brief

    Goals, constraints, codes and schedule. What needs deciding, and with what data.

  2. Step 2

    Parametric model

    The design becomes geometry with variables that can be tested.

  3. Step 3

    Analysis

    Simulation, fabrication checks or mapping of the process to automate.

  4. Step 4

    Optimization

    Options compared, trade-offs made visible, tools tested with the team.

  5. Step 5

    Delivery

    Drawings, report, cutting files, model and documented tool.
